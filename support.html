Mark support

Ready when you are.

Enable Mark in Safari, open any webpage, and select the Mark button to begin.

Enable the extension

  1. Open the Mark app after installation.
  2. Follow the on-screen steps to turn on Mark in Safari.
  3. Allow access for the websites you want to annotate.

Restore your subscription

Open the Mark app and select Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that started the subscription. Apple handles billing, cancellation, and renewal in Apple Account settings.

Find saved images

Mark saves images to the local or iCloud Drive location shown in the Library. Copies shared to Photos, Files, or another app remain there until you remove them from that destination.

Still need help?

Open Mark’s App Store page and choose App Support to contact spartadata. Include your device, operating system version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include private webpage content.
