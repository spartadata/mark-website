Where Mark lives in Safari

Mark uses Safari's native extension placement. On Mac it can live directly in the toolbar. On iPhone and iPad it appears as a named action in Safari's Page or More menu.

Safari controls the exact surrounding buttons and placement, which can vary by device, window size, toolbar layout, and OS version.

About this app

See it. Mark it. Ship it.

Mark is the visual feedback companion for Safari. Highlight copy, draw a clear marker line, outline the exact region, and leave a movable note beside the thing that needs attention. Then save the page and its annotations as one organized image.

Built for designers, developers, QA teams, and vibe coders working across live websites, local builds, SaaS products, and AI-generated experiences.
